<summary_of_elements>

### TASKS:
- **userTask("Enter Login Credentials")** - Customer provides username/password
- **serviceTask("Check Credentials")** - System validates login
- **userTask("Select Product")** - Customer chooses item
- **userTask("Add to Cart")** - Customer confirms product selection
- **serviceTask("Save Product to Cart")** - System persists selection
- **serviceTask("Check All Products Selected")** - System validates cart completeness
- **serviceTask("Finish Order")** - System finalizes order
- **userTask("Enter Payment Data")** - Customer provides payment info
- **receiveTask("Receive: bank confirmation")** - System waits for bank response
- **userTask("Enter Shipping Address")** - Customer provides delivery address
- **userTask("Enter Billing Address (if different)")** - Optional billing address
- **serviceTask("Finalize Order")** - System completes transaction

### GATEWAYS:
- **XOR** after credential check (success/failure)
- **XOR** after product selection check (complete/incomplete)
- **AND** after order finish (parallel payment/shipment prep)
- **XOR** after address entry (same/different billing)

### EVENTS:
- **startEvent("Customer Initiates Login")** - Manual trigger
- **endEvent("Order Completed")** - Normal completion
- **endEvent("Login Failed")** - Error termination

### BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- **timerBoundary("PT10M")** on receiveTask("Receive: bank confirmation")
  - SLA: Payment confirmation must arrive within 10 minutes
  - Handler: sendTask("Send: payment timeout notification")

### SUBPROCESSES:
- **Subprocess "Payment Processing"**
  - Purpose: Isolate payment validation and address collection
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping with internal parallelism

</summary_of_elements>

                receiveTask("Receive: bank confirmation"):
                    timerBoundary("PT10M"):
                        sendTask("Send: payment timeout notification")

                if (billing_different):
                    userTask("Enter Billing Address (if different)")

                endEvent("Payment Complete")
            endSubProcess

            serviceTask("Prepare Shipment")
        END_AND

        serviceTask("Finalize Order")
        endEvent("Order Completed")

    else:
        // Loop back to product selection
        -> userTask("Select Product")

else:
    endEvent("Login Failed")
