### TASKS:
- **serviceTask("Query Webshops for Parts Availability")** - Automated system queries all webshops for part availability, pricing, and delivery times.
- **scriptTask("Computation: Calculate Optimal Order Distribution")** - Computation to determine the cheapest parts and optimal distribution across webshops.
- **serviceTask("Place Orders with Webshops")** - Automated system places orders based on computed distribution.
- **receiveTask("Receive: Parts Delivery")** - Waits for parts to arrive in batches.
- **userTask("Start Building Contract")** - Human task to begin building once parts arrive.
- **scriptTask("Computation: Check Stock Levels")** - Computation to check stock levels for each part.
- **serviceTask("Reorder Parts (Cheapest)")** - Automated reorder if stock falls below 5.
- **serviceTask("Reorder Parts (Fastest)")** - Automated reorder if stock falls below 3.
- **sendTask("Send: Complaint Email to Friends")** - Sends a complaint email if stock reaches zero.

### GATEWAYS:
- **XOR at "Check Stock Levels"** - Conditions: `stock < 5`, `stock < 3`, `stock == 0`, default (no action).
- **AND at "Place Orders"** - Parallel branches for placing orders with multiple webshops.

### EVENTS:
- **startEvent("Contract Project Initiated")** - Manual trigger to start the process.
- **endEvent("Contract Project Completed")** - Normal completion of the process.
- **intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("2 days"))** - Waits for parts delivery with a 2-day buffer for variability.

### BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- **timerBoundary("2 days") on "Receive: Parts Delivery"** - SLA: Parts delivery variability of +/- 2 days.

### SUBPROCESSES:
- **Subprocess "Order Parts"** - Purpose: Handles the querying, computation, and ordering of parts.
  - Justification: Cohesive grouping of related tasks (querying, computation, ordering) with clear purpose and reusability.

- **Subprocess "Manage Stock and Reorders"** - Purpose: Handles stock level checks and reordering logic.
