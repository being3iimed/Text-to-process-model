<key_design_decisions>

## Key Design Decisions

### **1. Parallelism for Item Selection & Payment Setup**
- **Why AND Gateway?**
  The description states these actions occur *simultaneously* ("the user simultaneously selects the items... and sets a payment method").
  **BPMN Compliance**: Parallel gateways are the correct construct for independent concurrent tasks.

### **2. XOR for Payment Decision**
- **Why not OR?**
  The user either pays in full **or** sets up installments—these are mutually exclusive.
  **BPMN Compliance**: XOR gateways enforce exclusive branches.

### **3. Reward Selection After Items (But Independent of Payment)**
- **Placement Justification**:
  The reward depends on *purchase value* (from item selection) but is *independent of payment*.
  **Modeling Choice**: Placed after item selection but outside the payment XOR branch to reflect independence.

### **4. Return/Exchange Loop**
- **Why a Loop?**
  The description states: *"Every time items are returned, a new delivery is made."*
  **BPMN Compliance**: Loops are explicitly supported for repetitive actions. The loop exits when `user_requests_exchange` is false.

### **5. No Subprocesses**
- **Necessity Test Failed**:
  - No cohesive grouping of 3+ tasks with a single purpose.
  - No reuse or complexity isolation needed.
  **Compliance**: Avoids unnecessary abstraction.

### **6. No Boundary Events**
- **Gating Criteria Not Met**:
  - No hard SLAs (e.g., "delivery must complete in 2 days").
  - No expected error scenarios (e.g., "payment fails").
  **Compliance**: Boundary events omitted to prevent speculative design.

## Alternatives Considered (and Rejected)

### **1. Subprocess for "Delivery & Returns"**
- **Why Rejected?**
  The loop is simple (3 tasks) and tightly coupled to the main flow. A subprocess would add overhead without clarity benefits.
  **Validation**: Fails the **Three-Question Test** (Q2: Only 3 tasks; Q3: Removal doesn’t hurt readability).

### **2. OR Gateway for Payment**
- **Why Rejected?**
  The description implies mutual exclusivity ("either pays or completes an installment agreement").
  **BPMN Compliance**: OR gateways are for non-exclusive conditions (e.g., "if VIP **and** high-value order").

### **3. Timer Boundary on Delivery**
- **Why Rejected?**
  No SLA mentioned (e.g., "delivery must arrive in 48 hours").
  **Compliance**: Boundary events require explicit business requirements.

### **4. Separate Process for Returns**
- **Why Rejected?**
  Returns are part of the **same order lifecycle**. Separating would break traceability.
  **BPMN Best Practice**: Keep related activities in one process unless reuse is needed.
