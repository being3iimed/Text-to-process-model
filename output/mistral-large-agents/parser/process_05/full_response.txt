- **userTask("Issue Request for Proposals (RFP)")** - Human judgment required to draft and issue RFP
- **receiveTask("Receive Supplier Proposals")** - Waiting for external responses; no SLA mentioned → no timerBoundary
- **userTask("Evaluate Proposals")** - Human evaluation against criteria (cost, quality, delivery time)
- **userTask("Conduct Site Visits/Interviews")** - Optional human interaction (conditional)
- **userTask("Select Supplier")** - Human decision based on evaluation
- **userTask("Negotiate Contract Terms")** - Human negotiation process

- **startEvent("Need for New Supplier Identified")** - Trigger: Manual (business need)

if (site_visit_required):
    userTask("Conduct Site Visits/Interviews")
    if (supplier_meets_criteria):
        userTask("Select Supplier")
    else:
        endEvent("Process Terminated - No Suitable Supplier")
else:
    userTask("Select Supplier")

userTask("Negotiate Contract Terms")
userTask("Sign Contract")
serviceTask("Onboard Supplier")
