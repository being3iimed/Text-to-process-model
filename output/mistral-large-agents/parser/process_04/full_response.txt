## Assumptions and Clarifications Needed

1. **Campaign End Date**:
   - **Assumption**: The campaign has a fixed end date/time.
   - **Action**: Replace `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS` in `timerStartEvent` with the actual ISO 8601 datetime (e.g., `"2024-12-31T23:59:59"`).
   - **Question**: Should the end trigger be a **duration** (e.g., "30 days after launch") instead of a fixed date? If so, replace `timeDate` with `timeDuration` (e.g., `"P30D"`).

2. **Performance Tracking**:
   - **Assumption**: Metrics are tracked **automatically** in real-time.
   - **Question**: Are there manual reviews or adjustments during the campaign? If yes, add a `userTask("Review Mid-Campaign Performance")` after `Track Performance Metrics`.

3. **Lead Follow-Up**:
   - **Assumption**: Sales follow-up is a **single action**.
   - **Question**: Does the follow-up involve multiple steps (e.g., "Qualify Lead → Schedule Call → Send Proposal")? If yes, consider a subprocess for `Sales Follow-Up`.

4. **Error Scenarios**:
   - **Assumption**: No explicit failures (e.g., CRM downtime, ad platform errors).
   - **Question**: Should boundary events be added for:
     - `serviceTask("Launch Campaign")` → `errorBoundary`: "Ad platform failure" → `userTask("Manual Campaign Deployment")`?
     - `serviceTask("Collect Leads in CRM")` → `errorBoundary`: "CRM timeout" → `sendTask("Send: alert to IT team")`?

5. **Channel Selection**:
   - **Assumption**: Channels are selected **once** at the start.
   - **Question**: Can channels be adjusted mid-campaign based on performance? If yes, replace `userTask("Select Promotion Channels")` with a subprocess or add a loop.
