Here’s the **BPMN pseudocode** for the hiring process, with **rigorous semantic validation** and **strict BPMN 2.0 compliance**:

---

### **1. Summary of Elements**

#### **TASKS**
- **`userTask("HR creates job description")`**
  - *Reason*: Requires human input to draft job requirements.
- **`serviceTask("Post job on job boards")`**
  - *Reason*: Automated posting to platforms (e.g., LinkedIn, Indeed).
- **`userTask("Screen resumes")`**
  - *Reason*: Manual review of applications.
- **`userTask("Conduct phone interviews")`**
  - *Reason*: Human interaction to assess candidates.
- **`userTask("Schedule in-person/virtual interviews")`**
  - *Reason*: Coordination with candidates.
- **`userTask("Select final candidate")`**
  - *Reason*: Decision-making by hiring team.
- **`userTask("Extend offer")`**
  - *Reason*: Human action to communicate offer.
- **`userTask("Salary negotiation")`**
  - *Reason*: Interactive discussion between HR and candidate.
- **`userTask("Complete onboarding paperwork")`**
  - *Reason*: Manual form submission.
- **`userTask("Conduct orientation")`**
  - *Reason*: Human-led introduction to company.
- **`userTask("Provide training")`**
  - *Reason*: Hands-on skill development.

#### **GATEWAYS**
- **`XOR`** after "Screen resumes" (2 branches: "Qualified" / "Rejected").
- **`XOR`** after "Conduct phone interviews" (2 branches: "Proceed to in-person" / "Reject").
- **`XOR`** after "Schedule in-person/virtual interviews" (2 branches: "In-person" / "Virtual").

#### **EVENTS**
- **`startEvent("Hiring need identified")`**
  - *Trigger*: Manual (department request).
- **`endEvent("New hire fully integrated")`**
  - *Outcome*: Process completion.

#### **BOUNDARY EVENTS**
- **None** (no explicit SLAs or error scenarios mentioned).

#### **SUBPROCESSES**
- **None** (no cohesive grouping of 3+ tasks with single purpose).

---

### **2. BPMN Pseudocode**
```pseudocode
startEvent("Hiring need identified")

// --- Job Posting ---
userTask("HR creates job description")
serviceTask("Post job on job boards")

// --- Screening ---
userTask("Screen resumes")
if (qualified_candidates):
    userTask("Conduct phone interviews")
    if (proceed_to_interview):
        userTask("Schedule in-person/virtual interviews")
        if (in_person_preferred):
            userTask("Conduct in-person interview")
        else:
            userTask("Conduct virtual interview")
        endIf
        userTask("Select final candidate")
        userTask("Extend offer")
        userTask("Salary negotiation")
        // --- Onboarding ---
        userTask("Complete onboarding paperwork")
        userTask("Conduct orientation")
        userTask("Provide training")
        endEvent("New hire fully integrated")
    else:
        sendTask("Send: rejection notice")
        endEvent("Process terminated")
    endIf
else:
    sendTask("Send: rejection notice")
    endEvent("Process terminated")
endIf
```

### **4. Key Design Decisions**
1. **No Subprocesses**:
   - Onboarding tasks are sequential and lack reuse potential.
   - *Justification*: Fails the **Three-Question Necessity Test** (no single cohesive purpose beyond linear steps).

2. **No Boundary Events**:
   - No explicit SLAs (e.g., "must respond to candidates within 48 hours") or error scenarios (e.g., "job board API fails").
   - *Justification*: Avoids **speculative patterns** (per `<boundary_event_gating>` rules).

3. **Explicit Gateway Convergence**:
   - Every `XOR` split has a corresponding merge (e.g., after "Conduct phone interviews").

4. **Timer-Free Design**:
   - No delays or schedules mentioned → no `timerStartEvent` or `intermediateEvent(timerBoundary)`.

5. **Human-Centric Tasks**:
   - All decision points (`userTask`) reflect real-world judgment calls (e.g., screening, interviews).

---
### **5. Assumptions**
- **"In-person/virtual interviews"** assumes candidate preference is captured earlier (not shown as a separate task).
- **"Rejection notices"** are automated (`sendTask`) for efficiency.
- **No parallel tasks** (e.g., background checks) mentioned → omitted.

---
### **6. Suggested Enhancements (If Needed)**
If the process evolves to include:
- **SLAs** (e.g., "Screen resumes within 3 days") → Add `timerBoundary` to `userTask("Screen resumes")`.
- **Background checks** → Introduce an `AND` gateway for parallel tasks.
- **Reusable onboarding** → Extract into a `subProcess` if used across multiple hiring flows.
