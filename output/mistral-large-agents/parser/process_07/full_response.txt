## **Process Analysis: Travel Booking & Fulfillment**

### **1. Summary of Elements**

#### **TASKS**
- **`userTask("Search for Travel Options")`** – Customer searches for flights/trains/buses.
- **`userTask("Select Route, Date, and Time")`** – Customer chooses preferred option.
- **`userTask("Enter Personal & Payment Details")`** – Customer provides booking info.
- **`serviceTask("Generate Ticket")`** – System creates the ticket.
- **`serviceTask("Update Seat Inventory")`** – System adjusts available seats.
- **`sendTask("Send: Ticket via Email/SMS")`** – System delivers ticket to customer.
- **`sendTask("Send: Pre-Travel Reminders")`** – System sends check-in/boarding instructions.
- **`userTask("Complete Journey")`** – Customer finishes travel.
- **`userTask("Provide Feedback (Optional)")`** – Post-travel feedback.

#### **GATEWAYS**
- **None required** – Linear flow with no branching.

#### **EVENTS**
- **`startEvent("Customer Initiates Search")`** – Manual trigger.
- **`endEvent("Journey Completed")`** – Primary process end.
- **`endEvent("Feedback Received (Optional)")`** – Extended process end.

#### **BOUNDARY EVENTS**
- **None** – No explicit SLAs or error handling mentioned.

#### **SUBPROCESSES**
- **None** – All tasks are linear and cohesive.
