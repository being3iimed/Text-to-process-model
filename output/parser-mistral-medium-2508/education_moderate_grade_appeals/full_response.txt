- userTask("Professor reviews appeal") - Human judgment required
- userTask("Chair reviews escalated appeal") - Human judgment required
- serviceTask("Registrar updates grade system") - Automated system update
- sendTask("Send: professor's decision to student") - One-way communication
- sendTask("Send: chair's final decision to student") - One-way communication

GATEWAYS:
- XOR after professor review with 2 branches:
  1. Grade confirmed correct

- XOR after professor decision with 2 branches:
  1. Student accepts decision
  2. Student escalates to chair

- startEvent("Grade Appeal Process Initiated") - Manual trigger
- endEvent("Appeal Process Complete") - Normal completion
- timerStartEvent("10-day appeal window") - Time constraint for submission

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- timerBoundary("10 days") on initial submission - Hard SLA for appeal window

- None identified (all tasks are linear with clear dependencies)
