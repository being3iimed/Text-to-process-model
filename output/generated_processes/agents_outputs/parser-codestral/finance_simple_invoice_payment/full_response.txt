### Summary of Elements

```
TASKS:
- userTask("Finance Manager Approves Payment") - Requires human approval
- serviceTask("Log Invoice") - Automated system action
- serviceTask("Calculate Payment Due Date") - Automated calculation
- serviceTask("Generate Payment Request") - Automated system action
- serviceTask("Process Payment") - Automated bank transfer
- sendTask("Send: Payment Confirmation") - One-way communication

GATEWAYS:
- XOR at point X with condition: payment_approved

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Invoice Received") - Trigger: message (vendor invoice)
- endEvent("Payment Complete") - Normal completion
- timerStartEvent("Daily at 09:00") - Scheduled trigger

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- timerBoundary on "Generate Payment Request" - SLA: Payment must be generated on due date
- errorBoundary on "Process Payment" - Error: Bank transfer failure

SUBPROCESSES:
- Subprocess "Payment Processing" - Purpose: Handles payment generation, approval, and processing
- Justification: Three related tasks with cohesive purpose
```

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Invoice Received")
serviceTask("Log Invoice")
serviceTask("Calculate Payment Due Date")
intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("PT0S"))  # Immediate timer to trigger payment generation on due date
serviceTask("Generate Payment Request"):
    timerBoundary("PT0S"):
        serviceTask("Generate Payment Request")
userTask("Finance Manager Approves Payment")
if (payment_approved):
    serviceTask("Process Payment"):
        errorBoundary():
            userTask("Handle Payment Failure")
    sendTask("Send: Payment Confirmation")
else:
    serviceTask("Reject Payment Request")
endEvent("Payment Complete")
```
