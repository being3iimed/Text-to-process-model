### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- userTask("Confirm Dates and Room Availability") - Event planner confirms dates and room availability
- serviceTask("Calculate Required Setup") - System calculates required setup: room capacity, furniture configuration, AV equipment needed
- userTask("Prepare Menu Options") - Catering team receives event details and prepares menu options
- userTask("Approve Menu and Final Headcount") - Client approves menu and final headcount
- serviceTask("Prepare Food") - Catering prepares food day-before, stores appropriately, and prepares serving supplies
- serviceTask("Set Up Room") - Housekeeping sets up room with configured furniture
- serviceTask("Test AV Equipment") - IT team tests AV equipment
- serviceTask("Deliver Food and Set Up Buffet Area") - Catering team delivers food in thermal containers and sets up buffet area
- serviceTask("Break Down Setup and Restore Room") - Housekeeping breaks down setup and restores room to standard configuration

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at point X with conditions A, B, default

**EVENTS**:
- startEvent("Process Begins") - trigger: manual
- endEvent("Process Complete") - normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- None

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- None

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Process Begins")
userTask("Confirm Dates and Room Availability")
serviceTask("Calculate Required Setup")
userTask("Prepare Menu Options")
userTask("Approve Menu and Final Headcount")
serviceTask("Prepare Food")
AND:
    serviceTask("Set Up Room")
    serviceTask("Test AV Equipment")
    serviceTask("Deliver Food and Set Up Buffet Area")
END_AND
serviceTask("Break Down Setup and Restore Room")
endEvent("Process Complete")
```
