- userTask("Enter Vehicle Information") - Customer provides make, model, year, VIN, driving history
- serviceTask("Query Risk Database") - System retrieves risk rating and loss history
- scriptTask("Computation: Calculate Premium") - Computes premium based on vehicle value, driver age, violations, coverage type
- userTask("Review Quote") - Customer views generated quote
- userTask("Purchase Policy") - Customer completes purchase
- serviceTask("Save Quote") - System stores quote for later retrieval

- XOR after quote review with conditions:
  - "purchase_selected"
  - "save_selected" (default)

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Quote Process Initiated") - Manual trigger
- endEvent("Quote Purchased") - Successful purchase completion
- endEvent("Quote Saved") - Quote stored for later

- None (no explicit SLA requirements or error scenarios mentioned)

startEvent("Quote Process Initiated")
    userTask("Enter Vehicle Information")

    scriptTask("Computation: Calculate Premium")

        endEvent("Quote Purchased")
