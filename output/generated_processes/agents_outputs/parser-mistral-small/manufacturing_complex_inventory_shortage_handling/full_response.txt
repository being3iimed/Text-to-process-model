### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- serviceTask("Check Inventory for Component X") - Verify stock levels
- serviceTask("Check Next Supplier Delivery") - Determine delivery schedule
- serviceTask("Check for Alternative Components") - Find compatible alternatives
- userTask("Manager Approves Expedite") - Human approval for premium shipping
- serviceTask("Request Supplier Expedite") - Contact supplier for rush delivery
- sendTask("Send: Notify Production of Delay") - Inform production team
- sendTask("Send: Notify Dependent Lines") - Inform other production lines
- userTask("Procurement Manager Finds Backup Supplier") - Find alternative supplier
- serviceTask("Create Purchase Order") - Generate PO for backup supplier
- serviceTask("Log Cost Variance") - Record cost differences
- serviceTask("Update Supplier Metrics") - Adjust supplier performance data

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at "Delivery Time Check" with conditions:
  - delivery_within_24_hours
  - delivery_beyond_24_hours (default)
- XOR at "Alternative Component Check" with conditions:
  - alternative_available
  - no_alternative (default)
- XOR at "Expedite Success Check" with conditions:
  - expedite_successful
  - expedite_failed (default)

**EVENTS**:
- startEvent("Inventory Check Triggered") - Trigger: manual or automated
- endEvent("Production Resumes") - Normal completion
- intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("24 hours")) - Wait for supplier delivery

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- errorBoundary on "Request Supplier Expedite" - Error: supplier cannot expedite
- errorBoundary on "Create Purchase Order" - Error: backup supplier unavailable

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- Subprocess "Expedite Handling" - Purpose: Manage expedited shipping process
  - Justification: Cohesive shipping logic with multiple steps
- Subprocess "Backup Supplier Process" - Purpose: Find and engage backup supplier
  - Justification: Complex multi-step process with clear purpose

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Inventory Check Triggered")

serviceTask("Check Inventory for Component X")

if (inventory_sufficient):
    endEvent("Production Proceeds")
else:
    serviceTask("Check Next Supplier Delivery")

    if (delivery_within_24_hours):
        intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("24 hours"))
        endEvent("Production Resumes")
    else:
        serviceTask("Check for Alternative Components")

        if (alternative_available):
            sendTask("Send: Notify Production to Use Alternative")
            endEvent("Production Resumes")
        else:
            subProcess("Expedite Handling"):
                startEvent("Expedite Start")
                serviceTask("Request Supplier Expedite")
                    errorBoundary():
                        subProcess("Backup Supplier Process"):
                            startEvent("Backup Supplier Start")
                            userTask("Procurement Manager Finds Backup Supplier")
                            serviceTask("Create Purchase Order")
                                errorBoundary():
                                    userTask("Procurement Manager Finds Alternative Backup Supplier")
                            sendTask("Send: Confirm Backup Supplier Delivery")
                            endEvent("Backup Supplier Process Complete")
                        endSubProcess
                sendTask("Send: Notify Production of Expedite")
                if (expedite_successful):
                    endEvent("Expedite Complete")
                else:
                    endEvent("Proceed to Backup Supplier")
            endSubProcess

            sendTask("Send: Notify Dependent Production Lines of Potential Delay")
            serviceTask("Log Cost Variance")
            serviceTask("Update Supplier Metrics")
            endEvent("Production Resumes")
```
