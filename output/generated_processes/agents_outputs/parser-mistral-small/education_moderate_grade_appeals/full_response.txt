### Summary of Elements

**TASKS:**
- `userTask("Student submits grade appeal form")` - Student initiates appeal process
- `serviceTask("Professor reviews student's work, grading rubric, and appeal justification")` - Automated system action to route to professor
- `userTask("Professor confirms grade is correct and responds with explanation")` - Professor's decision path A
- `serviceTask("Professor adjusts grade and notifies registrar")` - Professor's decision path B
- `userTask("Student escalates to department chair")` - Student's escalation path
- `serviceTask("Chair reviews appeal and professor's response")` - Chair's review task
- `serviceTask("Chair makes final determination")` - Chair's decision task
- `sendTask("Send: outcome notification to student")` - Communicate final outcome
- `serviceTask("Registrar updates system")` - Update grade in system

**GATEWAYS:**
- XOR at professor's decision point with conditions:
  - Grade is correct (path A)
  - Grade is incorrect (path B)
- XOR at student's escalation decision point with conditions:
  - Student agrees (path A)
  - Student disagrees (path B)

**EVENTS:**
- `startEvent("Grade Appeal Process")` - Trigger: manual (student submission)
- `endEvent("Process Complete")` - Normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS:**
- `timerBoundary("10 days")` on `userTask("Student submits grade appeal form")` - SLA: Appeal must be submitted within 10 days of grade posting

**SUBPROCESSES:**
- None identified (all tasks are linear and directly related)
