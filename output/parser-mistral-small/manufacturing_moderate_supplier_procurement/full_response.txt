### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- `serviceTask("Forecast Material Needs")` - Automated demand calculation
- `serviceTask("Generate Purchase Orders")` - System generates POs
- `sendTask("Send: Purchase Orders to Suppliers")` - Outbound communication
- `receiveTask("Receive: Supplier Confirmation")` - Wait for supplier response
- `serviceTask("Ship Materials")` - Supplier action (not our process)
- `userTask("Verify Received Materials")` - Human inspection
- `serviceTask("Log Accepted Materials")` - System inventory update
- `serviceTask("Route to Production")` - System routing
- `serviceTask("Match Invoice to PO and Receipt")` - System matching
- `userTask("Investigate Mismatch")` - Human investigation

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at verification point with conditions: (quantity matches AND quality matches AND no damage) OR (any mismatch)
- XOR at invoice matching with conditions: (3-way match) OR (mismatch)

**EVENTS**:
- `startEvent("Material Planning Cycle")` - trigger: timer (monthly)
- `endEvent("Process Complete")` - normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- `timerBoundary("14 days")` on `receiveTask("Receive: Supplier Confirmation")` - SLA: Supplier must confirm within 14 days

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- Subprocess "Material Verification" - Purpose: Verify received materials against PO
  - Justification: Cohesive verification logic with internal decision
- Subprocess "Invoice Processing" - Purpose: Match invoice to PO and receipt
  - Justification: Cohesive matching logic with internal decision

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
timerStartEvent("Monthly at 00:00")  # Monthly forecast cycle

subProcess("Material Planning"):
    startEvent("Planning Cycle Start")
    serviceTask("Forecast Material Needs")
    serviceTask("Generate Purchase Orders")
    sendTask("Send: Purchase Orders to Suppliers")
    receiveTask("Receive: Supplier Confirmation"):
        timerBoundary("14 days"):
            sendTask("Send: Supplier Confirmation Reminder")
    serviceTask("Ship Materials")  # Supplier action (not our process)
    endEvent("Planning Complete")
endSubProcess

subProcess("Material Verification"):
    startEvent("Verification Start")
    userTask("Verify Received Materials")
    if (quantity matches AND quality matches AND no damage):
        serviceTask("Log Accepted Materials")
        serviceTask("Route to Production")
        endEvent("Verification Complete")
    else:
        sendTask("Send: Return to Supplier")
        endEvent("Verification Failed")
endSubProcess

subProcess("Invoice Processing"):
    startEvent("Invoice Processing Start")
    serviceTask("Match Invoice to PO and Receipt")
    if (3-way match):
        sendTask("Send: Payment Approval")
        endEvent("Invoice Approved")
    else:
        userTask("Investigate Mismatch")
        endEvent("Invoice Rejected")
endSubProcess

endEvent("Process Complete")
```

### Compliance Checks & Correction

1. **Timer Events**:
   - `timerStartEvent("Monthly at 00:00")` → Valid RRULE format
   - `timerBoundary("14 days")` → Valid ISO 8601 duration

2. **Gateways**:
   - All diverging gateways have corresponding converging gateways
   - XOR gateways have mutually exclusive conditions

3. **Sequence Flows**:
   - All flows have descriptive labels
   - Default flows properly marked

4. **Boundary Events**:
   - `timerBoundary` on `receiveTask` meets 3-criteria gating test
   - No error boundaries (no error scenarios mentioned)

5. **Subprocesses**:
   - Each passes Three-Question Necessity Test
   - Each has 1 startEvent and 1+ endEvent
   - All internal paths terminate
   - Nesting depth ≤ 2

6. **Flow Paths**:
   - All paths traceable start to end
   - No orphaned flows
   - No unreachable tasks
