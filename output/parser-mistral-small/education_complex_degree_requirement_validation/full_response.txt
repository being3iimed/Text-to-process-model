### Summary of Elements

**TASKS:**
- serviceTask("Audit Degree Requirements") - Verify all academic and administrative requirements
- serviceTask("Verify Course Completions") - Check all major and general education courses
- serviceTask("Verify Credit Count") - Ensure 120+ credits earned
- serviceTask("Verify Cumulative GPA") - Check minimum 2.0 GPA
- serviceTask("Verify Major GPA") - Check major-specific GPA minimum
- serviceTask("Verify Disciplinary Holds") - Ensure no active holds
- serviceTask("Verify Library Fees") - Ensure all fees paid
- serviceTask("Verify Visa Status") - Check for international students
- serviceTask("Generate Incomplete Report") - If requirements not met
- serviceTask("Prepare Diploma") - Generate diploma
- serviceTask("Place Cap-and-Gown Order") - Order graduation attire
- serviceTask("Assign Graduation Slot") - Assign ceremony slot
- serviceTask("Verify Attendance") - Check attendance at ceremony
- serviceTask("Confer Degree") - Officially confer degree
- serviceTask("Issue Transcript") - Send final transcript

**GATEWAYS:**
- XOR at point after requirements verification with conditions: all requirements met or missing requirements
- AND at point after graduation approval with parallel tasks: prepare diploma, place order, assign slot

**EVENTS:**
- startEvent("Graduation Check Requested") - Trigger: student request
- endEvent("Graduation Process Complete") - Normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS:**
- None (all requirements are explicit and deterministic)

**SUBPROCESSES:**
- Subprocess "Verify Academic Requirements" - Purpose: Verify all academic requirements
  - Justification: Cohesive grouping of academic verification tasks
- Subprocess "Verify Administrative Requirements" - Purpose: Verify all administrative requirements
  - Justification: Cohesive grouping of administrative verification tasks
- Subprocess "Graduation Preparation" - Purpose: Prepare for graduation ceremony
  - Justification: Cohesive grouping of graduation preparation tasks

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Graduation Check Requested")

subProcess("Verify Academic Requirements"):
    startEvent("Academic Verification Start")
    serviceTask("Verify Course Completions")
    serviceTask("Verify Credit Count")
    serviceTask("Verify Cumulative GPA")
    serviceTask("Verify Major GPA")
    endEvent("Academic Verification Complete")
endSubProcess

subProcess("Verify Administrative Requirements"):
    startEvent("Administrative Verification Start")
    serviceTask("Verify Disciplinary Holds")
    serviceTask("Verify Library Fees")
    serviceTask("Verify Visa Status")
    endEvent("Administrative Verification Complete")
endSubProcess

if (all requirements met):
    subProcess("Graduation Preparation"):
        startEvent("Graduation Preparation Start")
        AND:
            serviceTask("Prepare Diploma")
            serviceTask("Place Cap-and-Gown Order")
            serviceTask("Assign Graduation Slot")
        END_AND
        endEvent("Graduation Preparation Complete")
    endSubProcess

    serviceTask("Verify Attendance")
    serviceTask("Confer Degree")
    serviceTask("Issue Transcript")
else:
    serviceTask("Generate Incomplete Report")

endEvent("Graduation Process Complete")
```
