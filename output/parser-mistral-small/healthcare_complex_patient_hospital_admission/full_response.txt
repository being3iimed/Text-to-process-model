### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- userTask("Triage Assessment") - Human judgment required for acuity level
- serviceTask("Check Bed Availability") - System checks bed status
- userTask("Physician Assessment") - Human judgment required
- serviceTask("Order Tests") - Automated system action
- businessRuleTask("Rule: Determine Admission") - Complex business rule
- serviceTask("Check Bed Types") - System checks bed availability
- sendTask("Send: Notify Nursing Manager") - One-way communication
- userTask("Assign Bed") - Human judgment for bed assignment
- userTask("Transfer Patient") - Human action required
- serviceTask("Initiate Protocols") - Automated system action
- serviceTask("Medication Reconciliation") - System action
- serviceTask("Allergy Verification") - System action
- serviceTask("Pharmacy Review") - System action
- sendTask("Send: Notify Physician") - One-way communication
- userTask("Adjust Medication Regimen") - Human action required
- serviceTask("Track Insurance Authorization") - System action
- sendTask("Send: Contact Patient") - One-way communication

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at point "Acuity and Bed Availability" with conditions:
  - acuity <= 2 AND bed available → admit to monitored bed
  - acuity > 2 OR no bed available → wait in ED area
- XOR at point "Admission Decision" with conditions:
  - needs admission → proceed to bed assignment
  - discharge → end process
- XOR at point "Bed Assignment" with conditions:
  - ICU bed available → assign ICU bed
  - General ward bed available → assign general ward bed
  - No bed available → notify nursing manager

**EVENTS**:
- startEvent("Patient Arrival") - Trigger: Patient presents to ED
- endEvent("Patient Discharged") - Normal completion
- intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("4 hours")) - On "Wait in ED Area" task

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- timerBoundary on "Wait in ED Area" - SLA: Patient must be reassessed every 4 hours
- errorBoundary on "Check Bed Availability" - Error: System failure

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- Subprocess "Admission Protocols" - Purpose: Handle medication and allergy verification
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of related tasks
- Subprocess "Insurance Authorization" - Purpose: Handle insurance verification and patient contact
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of related tasks
