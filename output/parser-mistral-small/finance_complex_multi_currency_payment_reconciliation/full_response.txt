### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- serviceTask("Convert currency to base currency") - System converts all amounts to base currency using daily exchange rates
- serviceTask("Match payment to outstanding invoice") - System matches each payment to an outstanding invoice
- serviceTask("Record payment as complete") - System records payment as complete
- serviceTask("Mark invoice as partially paid") - System marks invoice as partially paid and holds remaining balance
- serviceTask("Record overpayment as credit") - System records overpayment as credit for future invoices
- serviceTask("Hold payment as unmatched") - System holds payment as unmatched
- sendTask("Send: notification to accounting team") - System notifies accounting team to investigate
- userTask("Accounting team researches payment") - Accounting team researches whether payment is for old invoice, contains reference number, or is duplicate payment
- serviceTask("Update accounts receivable aging report") - System updates accounts receivable aging report
- serviceTask("Trigger automatic follow-up for overdue invoices") - System triggers automatic follow-up for overdue invoices

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at payment matching with conditions:
  - payment matches invoice amount exactly
  - payment is less than invoice amount
  - payment is more than invoice amount
  - payment doesn't match any invoice

**EVENTS**:
- startEvent("Daily Payments Received") - Trigger: timerStartEvent("Daily at 09:00")
- endEvent("Process Complete") - Normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- None identified that meet gating criteria

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- Subprocess "Payment Reconciliation" - Purpose: Handle payment matching and reconciliation
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of payment processing steps

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
timerStartEvent("Daily at 09:00")
serviceTask("Convert currency to base currency")
serviceTask("Match payment to outstanding invoice")

if (payment matches invoice amount exactly):
    serviceTask("Record payment as complete")
else if (payment is less than invoice amount):
    serviceTask("Mark invoice as partially paid")
else if (payment is more than invoice amount):
    serviceTask("Record overpayment as credit")
else:
    serviceTask("Hold payment as unmatched")
    sendTask("Send: notification to accounting team")
    userTask("Accounting team researches payment")

serviceTask("Update accounts receivable aging report")
serviceTask("Trigger automatic follow-up for overdue invoices")
endEvent("Process Complete")
```
