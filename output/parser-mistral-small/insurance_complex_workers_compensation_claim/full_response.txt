### Summary of Elements

**TASKS:**
- `userTask("Worker Reports Injury")` - Worker reports injury to employer
- `serviceTask("Submit Injury Report")` - Employer submits report to insurer
- `userTask("Assign Claims Adjuster")` - System assigns adjuster
- `sendTask("Send: Initial Contact")` - Adjuster contacts worker
- `userTask("Collect Injury Details")` - Adjuster collects details
- `serviceTask("Verify Work-Related")` - Adjuster determines coverage
- `serviceTask("Determine Benefits")` - Adjuster determines applicable benefits
- `serviceTask("Verify Wage History")` - Adjuster verifies wage/employment
- `userTask("Authorize Medical Treatment")` - Adjuster authorizes treatment
- `serviceTask("Review Medical Reports")` - Adjuster reviews reports
- `serviceTask("Monitor Recovery")` - Adjuster monitors recovery status
- `userTask("Order IME")` - Adjuster orders IME if dispute
- `userTask("Conduct IME")` - IME physician provides second opinion
- `serviceTask("Coordinate Job Placement")` - Adjuster coordinates with employer
- `sendTask("Send: Status Report")` - Adjuster sends status to board
- `serviceTask("Calculate Disability Award")` - Adjuster calculates award
- `serviceTask("Close Claim File")` - System closes claim file

**GATEWAYS:**
- XOR at "Determine Coverage" with conditions: work-related, not work-related
- XOR at "Determine Benefits" with conditions: medical, disability, both
- XOR at "Monitor Recovery" with conditions: can return to work, cannot return
- XOR at "IME Decision" with conditions: IME ordered, no IME needed

**EVENTS:**
- `startEvent("Injury Reported")` - Trigger: manual (worker reports injury)
- `endEvent("Claim Closed")` - Normal completion
- `intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("24 hours"))` - Employer must submit report within 24 hours

**BOUNDARY EVENTS:**
- `timerBoundary("24 hours")` on "Submit Injury Report" - SLA: regulatory requirement
- `errorBoundary()` on "Submit Injury Report" - Error: submission failure

**SUBPROCESSES:**
- Subprocess "Injury Assessment" - Purpose: Determine coverage and benefits
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping (coverage + benefits)
- Subprocess "Recovery Monitoring" - Purpose: Monitor worker recovery
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping (treatment + reports + return-to-work)
- Subprocess "Dispute Resolution" - Purpose: Handle injury severity disputes
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping (IME + second opinion)
