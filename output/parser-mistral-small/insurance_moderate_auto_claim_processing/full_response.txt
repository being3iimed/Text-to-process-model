### Summary of Elements

**TASKS**:
- `userTask("Collect Claim Details")` - Agent collects accident details from claimant
- `userTask("Request Documentation")` - Agent requests photos, police report, medical records
- `serviceTask("Validate Coverage")` - System checks policy period and damage coverage
- `serviceTask("Forward Claim to Appraiser")` - System routes claim to appraiser
- `userTask("Inspect Vehicle")` - Appraiser physically inspects vehicle
- `serviceTask("Estimate Repair Costs")` - Appraiser calculates repair estimate
- `userTask("Review Appraisal")` - Claims adjuster reviews appraisal report
- `serviceTask("Check Policy Limits")` - System verifies if repair cost within limits
- `sendTask("Send: Approval Notification")` - System notifies insured of approval
- `sendTask("Send: Exceeds Limit Notification")` - System notifies insured of limit exceeded
- `userTask("Select Repair Shop")` - Insured chooses repair shop
- `sendTask("Send: Repair Authorization")` - Insurer sends authorization to shop
- `userTask("Complete Repairs")` - Repair shop performs repairs
- `sendTask("Send: Invoice")` - Shop submits invoice to insurer
- `serviceTask("Verify Invoice")` - System checks invoice against estimate
- `sendTask("Send: Payment")` - System pays invoice if reasonable
- `sendTask("Send: Repair Completion Notification")` - System notifies insured of completion

**GATEWAYS**:
- XOR at "Review Appraisal" with conditions:
  - `repair_cost <= policy_limit` → Approval path
  - `repair_cost > policy_limit` → Exceeds limit path
- XOR at "Verify Invoice" with conditions:
  - `invoice matches estimate` → Payment path
  - `invoice does not match estimate` → Rejection path

**EVENTS**:
- `startEvent("Claim Submitted")` - Trigger: Claimant submits claim
- `endEvent("Claim Processed")` - Normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS**:
- `timerBoundary("4 hours")` on `userTask("Collect Claim Details")` - SLA: Must complete within 4 hours
- `errorBoundary()` on `serviceTask("Validate Coverage")` - Error: Policy data unavailable

**SUBPROCESSES**:
- Subprocess "Appraisal Process" - Purpose: Handle vehicle inspection and cost estimation
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of appraisal activities
- Subprocess "Repair Process" - Purpose: Manage repair shop authorization and payment
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of repair activities
