### Summary of Elements

**TASKS:**
- `userTask("Client Requests Conference Room and Catering")` - Human input to initiate the process
- `serviceTask("System Calculates Setup Requirements")` - Automated calculation of room capacity, furniture, AV equipment
- `sendTask("Send: Event Details to Catering")` - Communication to catering team
- `userTask("Client Approves Menu and Headcount")` - Human approval step
- `serviceTask("Catering Prepares Food Day Before")` - Automated preparation of food
- `serviceTask("Housekeeping Sets Up Room")` - Automated setup of room
- `serviceTask("IT Team Tests AV Equipment")` - Automated testing of AV equipment
- `serviceTask("Catering Delivers and Sets Up Buffet")` - Automated delivery and setup of food
- `serviceTask("Housekeeping Breaks Down Setup")` - Automated breakdown of setup

**GATEWAYS:**
- `XOR` at point after "Client Approves Menu and Headcount" with conditions "approved" and "rejected (default)"

**EVENTS:**
- `startEvent("Client Requests Conference Room and Catering")` - Trigger: manual initiation
- `endEvent("Event Complete")` - Normal completion

**BOUNDARY EVENTS:**
- None identified as all tasks have deterministic outcomes

**SUBPROCESSES:**
- `subProcess("Catering Preparation")` - Purpose: Handle all catering-related tasks
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of catering tasks
- `subProcess("Room Setup")` - Purpose: Handle all room setup tasks
  - Justification: Cohesive logical grouping of room setup tasks

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Client Requests Conference Room and Catering")
userTask("Client Requests Conference Room and Catering")
serviceTask("System Calculates Setup Requirements")
if (room_available AND catering_available):
    sendTask("Send: Event Details to Catering")
    userTask("Client Approves Menu and Headcount")
    if (approved):
        subProcess("Catering Preparation"):
            startEvent("Catering Preparation Start")
            serviceTask("Catering Prepares Food Day Before")
            serviceTask("Catering Stores Food Appropriately")
            serviceTask("Catering Prepares Serving Supplies")
            endEvent("Catering Preparation Complete")
        endSubProcess
        subProcess("Room Setup"):
            startEvent("Room Setup Start")
            serviceTask("Housekeeping Sets Up Room")
            serviceTask("IT Team Tests AV Equipment")
            serviceTask("Catering Delivers and Sets Up Buffet")
            endEvent("Room Setup Complete")
        endSubProcess
        endEvent("Event Complete")
    else:
        sendTask("Send: Rejection Notice to Client")
        endEvent("Event Rejected")
else:
    sendTask("Send: Unavailability Notice to Client")
    endEvent("Event Unavailable")
```
