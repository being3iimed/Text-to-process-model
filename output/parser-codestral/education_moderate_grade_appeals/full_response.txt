### Summary of Elements

```
TASKS:
- userTask("Student submits grade appeal form") - Student submits form within 10 days
- userTask("Professor reviews student's work and appeal justification") - Professor evaluates evidence
- userTask("Professor confirms grade is correct") - Professor defends original grade
- userTask("Professor adjusts grade") - Professor finds error and corrects grade
- userTask("Student escalates to department chair") - Student disagrees with professor's decision
- userTask("Chair reviews appeal and professor's response") - Chair evaluates all evidence
- userTask("Chair makes final determination") - Chair decides final grade
- serviceTask("Registrar updates system") - System records final grade

GATEWAYS:
- XOR at point after professor's review with conditions:
  - "Professor confirms grade is correct"
  - "Professor adjusts grade"
  - default (student escalates)

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Grade appeal process begins") - manual trigger
- endEvent("Appeal process complete") - normal completion

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- timerBoundary on "Student submits grade appeal form" - SLA: 10 days from grade posting
```

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Grade appeal process begins")

userTask("Student submits grade appeal form"):
    timerBoundary("10 days"):
        endEvent("Appeal period expired")

userTask("Professor reviews student's work and appeal justification")

if (professor_confirms_grade_correct):
    userTask("Professor confirms grade is correct")
    endEvent("Appeal process complete")
else if (professor_adjusts_grade):
    userTask("Professor adjusts grade")
    serviceTask("Registrar updates system")
    endEvent("Appeal process complete")
else:
    userTask("Student escalates to department chair")
    userTask("Chair reviews appeal and professor's response")
    userTask("Chair makes final determination")
    serviceTask("Registrar updates system")
    endEvent("Appeal process complete")
```
