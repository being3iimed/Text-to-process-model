## Process Elements Summary

### Tasks

1. **userTask**: "Set up tax calendar"
   - Justification: Requires human decision-making to establish deadlines and tracking mechanisms

2. **serviceTask**: "Categorize and record financial transactions"
   - Justification: Automated accounting software handles this routine task

3. **userTask**: "Reconcile accounts"
   - Justification: Requires human review and verification of financial records

4. **userTask**: "Review deductions"
   - Justification: Requires human judgment to assess eligibility and accuracy of deductions

5. **userTask**: "Prepare necessary documents"
   - Justification: Requires human compilation of information for filing

6. **serviceTask**: "Submit tax returns electronically"
   - Justification: Automated system handles electronic submission

7. **serviceTask**: "Save confirmation receipts"
   - Justification: Automated system saves digital receipts

8. **userTask**: "Provide additional documentation"
   - Justification: Requires human preparation of supporting documents for audit/inquiry

9. **userTask**: "Incorporate lessons learned"
   - Justification: Requires human reflection and planning for future improvements

### Gateways

1. **XOR Gateway**: "Audit or inquiry?"
   - Justification: Simple binary decision (yes/no) about whether audit/inquiry occurs

### Events

1. **startEvent**: "Fiscal year begins"
   - Justification: Process begins at start of fiscal year

2. **endEvent**: "All compliance obligations fulfilled"
   - Justification: Process ends when all requirements are met

### Boundary Events

1. **timerBoundary**: "1 month before deadline"
   - Justification: Hard SLA requirement to ensure timely preparation
   - Attached to: "Prepare necessary documents"
   - Handler: "Send reminder to accountant"

2. **errorBoundary**: "Submission fails"
   - Justification: Known fault pattern with deterministic recovery
   - Attached to: "Submit tax returns electronically"
   - Handler: "userTask: Resubmit tax returns"

## BPMN Pseudocode

```
startEvent("Fiscal year begins")
userTask("Set up tax calendar")
serviceTask("Categorize and record financial transactions")
userTask("Reconcile accounts")
userTask("Review deductions")
userTask("Prepare necessary documents"):
    timerBoundary("1 month before deadline"):
        sendTask("Send: reminder to accountant")
serviceTask("Submit tax returns electronically"):
    errorBoundary():
        userTask("Resubmit tax returns")
serviceTask("Save confirmation receipts")
if (audit_or_inquiry_occurs):
    userTask("Provide additional documentation")
endEvent("All compliance obligations fulfilled")
```
