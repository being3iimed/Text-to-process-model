### SUMMARY OF ELEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASKS:
- userTask("Doctor Prescribes Medication") - Human judgment required for prescription
- serviceTask("Send Prescription Electronically") - Automated system action to send prescription
- userTask("Pharmacy Technician Checks Insurance") - Human judgment required for insurance check
- serviceTask("Contact Insurance Company") - Automated system action to contact insurance
- userTask("Fill Prescription") - Human action to fill prescription
- serviceTask("Notify Patient to Pick Up") - Automated system action to notify patient
- userTask("Notify Patient and Doctor of Denial") - Human action to notify patient and doctor
- userTask("Patient Appeals Denial") - Human action for patient to appeal denial
- userTask("Doctor Prescribes Alternative") - Human judgment required for alternative prescription
- userTask("Patient Picks Up Prescription") - Human action for patient to pick up prescription

GATEWAYS:
- XOR at point X with conditions "Prior Authorization Required", "Prior Authorization Not Required", default
- XOR at point Y with conditions "Insurance Approves", "Insurance Denies", default

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Process Begins") - trigger: manual
- endEvent("Process Complete") - normal completion

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- None

SUBPROCESSES:
- None

### BPMN PSEUDOCODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
startEvent("Process Begins")
userTask("Doctor Prescribes Medication")
serviceTask("Send Prescription Electronically")
userTask("Pharmacy Technician Checks Insurance")
if (Prior Authorization Required):
    serviceTask("Contact Insurance Company")
    if (Insurance Approves):
        userTask("Fill Prescription")
        serviceTask("Notify Patient to Pick Up")
        userTask("Patient Picks Up Prescription")
    else:
        userTask("Notify Patient and Doctor of Denial")
        OR:
            userTask("Patient Appeals Denial")
            userTask("Doctor Prescribes Alternative")
        END_OR
else:
    userTask("Fill Prescription")
    serviceTask("Notify Patient to Pick Up")
    userTask("Patient Picks Up Prescription")
endEvent("Process Complete")
