### Summary of Elements

```
TASKS:
- serviceTask("Forecast Material Needs") - Automated calculation based on production schedule
- serviceTask("Generate Purchase Orders") - System creates PO documents
- receiveTask("Receive Supplier Confirmation") - Supplier responds with availability and delivery date
- serviceTask("Verify Material Quality") - Warehouse staff inspects materials
- serviceTask("Log Materials into Inventory") - System updates inventory records
- serviceTask("Route Materials to Production") - Warehouse staff directs materials to production area
- serviceTask("Match Invoice to PO and Receipt") - Finance team verifies invoice matches PO and receipt
- userTask("Investigate Mismatch") - Finance team reviews discrepancy

GATEWAYS:
- XOR at point X with conditions: quantity matches PO, quality matches specifications, no damage during shipping
- XOR at point Y with conditions: invoice matches PO and receipt, default

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Process Begins") - trigger: manual (forecasting schedule)
- endEvent("Process Complete") - normal completion

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- None (no explicit SLA or error scenarios mentioned)

SUBPROCESSES:
- None (no cohesive logical groupings identified)
```

### BPMN Pseudocode

```pseudocode
startEvent("Process Begins")
serviceTask("Forecast Material Needs")
serviceTask("Generate Purchase Orders")
sendTask("Send: Purchase Orders to Suppliers")
receiveTask("Receive Supplier Confirmation")
serviceTask("Verify Material Quality")
if (quantity matches PO AND quality matches specifications AND no damage during shipping):
    serviceTask("Log Materials into Inventory")
    serviceTask("Route Materials to Production")
    endEvent("Process Complete")
else:
    sendTask("Send: Rejection Notice to Supplier")
    endEvent("Process Complete")
serviceTask("Match Invoice to PO and Receipt")
if (invoice matches PO and receipt):
    serviceTask("Approve Invoice for Payment")
    endEvent("Process Complete")
else:
    userTask("Investigate Mismatch")
    endEvent("Process Complete")
```
