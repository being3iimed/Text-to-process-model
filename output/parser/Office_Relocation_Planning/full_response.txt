Here's the BPMN pseudocode for the office relocation process, following the canonical Pseudocode-to-JSON Mapping and emphasizing balanced, core-focused BPMN design:

```
startEvent("Office Relocation Process Begins")

# Location Selection Phase
userTask("Select new office location based on space requirements and proximity to clients")

# Committee Formation Phase
serviceTask("Form relocation committee to oversee logistics")
AND:
    serviceTask("Assign furniture movers")
    serviceTask("Assign IT setup team")
    serviceTask("Assign communication team")
END_AND

# Timeline Creation Phase
serviceTask("Create detailed relocation timeline")
serviceTask("Assign tasks to various teams")

# Moving Day Preparation
serviceTask("Notify all employees of moving day")
serviceTask("Prepare office for temporary pause")

# Moving Day Execution
serviceTask("Pause operations temporarily")
serviceTask("Transport equipment and documents")
serviceTask("Unpack and set up furniture")

# System Setup Phase
serviceTask("Set up IT systems and networks")
serviceTask("Test all systems for functionality")

# Final Verification
serviceTask("Verify all items are unpacked")
serviceTask("Ensure systems are operational")
serviceTask("Notify employees to resume work")

endEvent("Office Relocation Complete")
```
