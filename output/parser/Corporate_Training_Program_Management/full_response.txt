### Process Elements Summary

1. **userTask("Identify Skill Gaps")**: Identify skill gaps through surveys or performance reviews
2. **serviceTask("Develop Training Plan")**: Develop training plan including topics, schedules, and resources
3. **userTask("Enroll Employees")**: Enroll employees in relevant courses
4. **serviceTask("Track Attendance and Progress")**: Track attendance and progress during training
5. **serviceTask("Administer Assessments")**: Administer assessments to measure learning outcomes
6. **userTask("Collect Feedback")**: Collect feedback post-training to evaluate effectiveness
7. **serviceTask("Update Skill Records")**: Update skill records based on training outcomes
8. **serviceTask("Recommend Future Training")**: Recommend future training initiatives

1. **XOR Gateway**: Determine if additional training is needed based on assessment results

2. **endEvent("Process Complete")**: Process ends after updating skill records and recommending future training

None identified in this process.
