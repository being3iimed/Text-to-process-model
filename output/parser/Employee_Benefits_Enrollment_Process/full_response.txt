### Process Elements Summary

**Tasks**:
- userTask("Employee Reviews Benefits"): Employee reviews available benefits
- userTask("Employee Submits Choices"): Employee submits benefit selections
- serviceTask("HR Verifies Selections"): HR verifies employee selections
- serviceTask("HR Updates Payroll Records"): HR updates payroll records
- sendTask("Send: Confirmation Letter"): HR sends confirmation letter to employee

**Gateways**:
- None required for this simple linear process

**Events**:
- startEvent("Open Enrollment Begins"): Process starts at the beginning of open enrollment
- endEvent("Benefits Effective"): Process ends when benefits take effect

**Boundary Events**:
- None required for this process

**Subprocesses**:
- None required for this simple process

### BPMN Pseudocode

```
startEvent("Open Enrollment Begins")
userTask("Employee Reviews Benefits")
userTask("Employee Submits Choices")
serviceTask("HR Verifies Selections")
serviceTask("HR Updates Payroll Records")
sendTask("Send: Confirmation Letter")
endEvent("Benefits Effective")
```
