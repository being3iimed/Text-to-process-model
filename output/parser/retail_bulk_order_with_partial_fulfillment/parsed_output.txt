### SUMMARY OF ELEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TASKS:
- userTask("Manager Submits Bulk Order") - initial order submission
- serviceTask("Check Inventory Availability") - verify stock levels for all products
- userTask("Manager Reviews Shortage") - decide on partial shipment or wait
- serviceTask("Reserve Order") - mark order as reserved
- serviceTask("Schedule Packing") - prepare order for shipment
- serviceTask("Split Order") - separate available and backordered items
- serviceTask("Ship Available Items") - send immediately available items
- serviceTask("Hold Order") - keep order pending
- serviceTask("Ship Backorder Items") - send remaining items when stock arrives

GATEWAYS:
- XOR at point after inventory check with conditions "all products have sufficient stock" and "any product has insufficient stock"
- XOR at point after manager review with conditions "proceed with partial shipment" and "wait for full stock"

EVENTS:
- startEvent("Process Begins") - manual trigger
- endEvent("Order Processed") - normal completion
- intermediateEvent - timer delay for backorder items

BOUNDARY EVENTS:
- None identified in this process

SUBPROCESSES:
- None identified in this process

### BPMN PSEUDOCODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
startEvent("Process Begins")
userTask("Manager Submits Bulk Order")
serviceTask("Check Inventory Availability")

if (all products have sufficient stock):
    serviceTask("Reserve Order")
    serviceTask("Schedule Packing")
    endEvent("Order Processed")
else:
    userTask("Manager Reviews Shortage")

    if (proceed with partial shipment):
        serviceTask("Split Order")
        serviceTask("Ship Available Items")
        intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("until backorder items arrive"))
        serviceTask("Ship Backorder Items")
        endEvent("Order Processed")
    else:
        serviceTask("Hold Order")
        intermediateEvent(timerBoundary("until all items in stock"))
        serviceTask("Schedule Packing")
        endEvent("Order Processed")
