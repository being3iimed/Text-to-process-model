### Process Elements Summary

#### Tasks

1. **userTask("Conduct Quality Audit")**: Audit team visits supplier facility to inspect production processes, test samples, and review documentation
2. **userTask("Document Findings")**: Audit team documents findings and recommends corrective actions if issues are identified
3. **userTask("Implement Corrective Actions")**: Supplier implements recommended changes and submits evidence of compliance
4. **userTask("Conduct Follow-up Audit")**: Audit team conducts follow-up audit to verify improvements
5. **userTask("Evaluate Compliance")**: Audit team evaluates supplier's compliance with corrective actions

#### Gateways

1. **XOR Gateway**: Determines if issues were identified during initial audit
2. **XOR Gateway**: Determines if follow-up audit is needed
3. **XOR Gateway**: Determines if supplier passes audit or is disqualified

#### Events

1. **startEvent("Supplier Flagged for Audit")**: Process begins when supplier is flagged for a routine quality audit
2. **endEvent("Supplier Passes Audit")**: Process ends when supplier passes the audit
3. **endEvent("Supplier Disqualified")**: Process ends when supplier is disqualified due to non-compliance

#### Subprocesses

None identified in this process.
