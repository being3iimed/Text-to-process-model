<elements>
Tasks:
- Customer Inquires (userTask - customer action)
- Collect Information (userTask - sales/customer support action)
- Address Concerns (userTask - sales/customer support action)
- Guide Product Selection (userTask - sales action)
- Provide Quote (userTask - sales action)
- Approve Quote (userTask - customer action)
- Place Order (userTask - sales action)
- Record Order (serviceTask - automatic system action)
- Send Confirmation (sendTask - outbound notification)

Gateways:
- XOR (exactly one path: customer interested or not interested)

Boundary Events:
- None (no timeout or error handling specified)

Events:
- Start (manual initiation by customer inquiry)
- End (normal completion after order confirmation)

Complexity: Simple
</elements>

<pseudocode>
startEvent("Customer Inquiry Process Start")
userTask("Customer inquires about product/service")
userTask("Sales/Customer Support collects relevant information")
userTask("Address any concerns or questions")

if (customer is interested):
    userTask("Guide customer through product/service selection")
    userTask("Sales representative provides quote")
    userTask("Customer approves quote")
    userTask("Sales representative places order")
    serviceTask("System records order")
    sendTask("Send order confirmation to customer")
else:
    userTask("Sales representative follows up with customer")

endEvent("Customer Inquiry Process End")
</pseudocode>
